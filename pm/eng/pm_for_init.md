
# Memo for init

## 1 Formalities

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this memo is to regulate the committee "init".

The purpose of init is to organize and carry out programming activities with a focus on developing software systems to streamline the chapter's work or help the chapters members, as well to be responsible for the operation, maintenance, and further development of these systems. The committee may also conduct activities with the purpose of educating. 

### 1.2 History

Created: 2025-11-XX

Last revision: 2025-11-XX 

### 1.3 Revising this Memo

In order to pass a revision of this Memo, a decision has to be made with a qualified majority at a chapter meeting.

## 2 Organisation

The committee is made up by it's board and members that are appointed by it's board.

init's board is made up by:

- Bootloader
- Kernel

These are elected at a chapter meeting.

Inits board may elect more board members. 
A chapter member may only be part of one board at a time.

### 2.1 Bootloader

Convenor and Chairman of init is Bootloader, who is responsible for ensuring that meetings with the whole committee is held regularly.

This person shall work long-term and strategically on developing the chapters digital systems and ensuring that they're maintained.


### 2.2 Kernel

Kernel shall in the absence of the Bootloader carry out their duties and execute their responsibilities.

The Kernel is also responsible for the work delegated to them by the Bootloader by mutual agreement.

## 3 Activities

### 3.1 Digital systems

init is responsible for:

- Development of digital systems
- Maintenance of digital systems
- Operation of digital systems

### 3.2 Programming activities

init is responsible for running events such as:

- Programmingevenings
- Workshops
- Other events in close connection to programming/digital systems.

## 4 Economy

The committee has no economy of its own.
